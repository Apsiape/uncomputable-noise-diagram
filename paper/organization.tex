\section{Organization of the simulation}\label{sec:organization}

We give the deterministic argument for a fixed assignment of read-only operands. In applying the result to the single rule, Section~\ref{sec:environment} specifies the local comparison in which those operands are fixed. All coordinates in a one-rank proof are child coordinates unless explicitly rescaled. Tiles, properness and nested rectangles adapt G\'acs \cite[Sections~10--11, including Lemma~11.2]{gacs86}; weak then strong computation follows the organization of his Lemmas~14.1--14.2. Ordinary relaxation, gap filling and the damage/recovery bounds adapt his Sections~12 (Healing) and~13 (Integrity of blocks). Terminal separation and end relief adapt his Sections~15 and~16, respectively.

\begin{definition}[Observations on a tile]\label{def:observations}
Use all translates of the grid of half-open \(j\)-tiles of width \(P^j\) and duration \(T^j\) in physical units. At rank zero the nominal and served observations are both the physical data value. At rank \(j>0\) a matching child is a live served child of the expected clock and address residues for this grid, including legal extended addresses of that residue. On the bottom face define \(x_j\) to be the unique live state \(s\) for which the three central fifths match the encoded packet \(\delta_{j-1}(s)\) and the core
\[
C_*=[12A+4q,P-12A-4q)
\]
is occupied by matching children, allowing a single common interval of at most \(2q\) erroneous or missing positions for both tests. Otherwise put \(x_j=\Dead\). Uniqueness follows because two such exceptional intervals together cannot hide all three homologous copies of any one letter, since \(2q<P_0\), and because packets are parsed literally (Section~\ref{sec:packets}); the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:classification} gives the details. The three central fifths form \([P_0,4P_0)\), which lies in \(C_*\). At child date \(s_c\) of that tile, that is \(s_c\) child ticks after its bottom face, set \(g_j=1\) if more than \(P/2\) of the \(P\) child positions of its own home carry matching children, and \(g_j=0\) otherwise. The served observation is \(y_j=x_j\) when \(g_j=1\) and \(y_j=\Dead\) otherwise. Call a tile computing if \(g_j=1\). The nominal output \(x_j'\) is the nominal observation at the following boundary of the same home.
\end{definition}

Use the same-origin child grid inside each tile. The bottom observation uses child date \(0\), and the service test uses child date \(s_c\), with \(0\le s_c<T\) since the service date lies in early computation (Section~\ref{sec:clock}). By induction the physical queries of \(x_j,y_j\) stay inside the spatial tile and have lookahead bounded by \(b_0=0\) and
\[
b_j\le s_cT^{j-1}+b_{j-1}\le T^j-1 .
\]
The observations are defined before choosing the sparse test.

\begin{definition}[Properness and protection]\label{def:proper}
A tile is proper dead if its matching bottom occupancy fits in a span of width \(q/2\). It is incoming-clear if every tile of a different rank-\(j\) origin whose time interval overlaps it and whose spatial home meets its \(64A P^{j-1}\) horizontal pad is proper dead. A tile is proper live if it is nominally live and incoming-clear and has matching bottom occupancy at all positions of \([1,P-1)\) except possibly one interval of width at most \(2q\). A nominal group uses the residues of \(x_j\); a served group uses the residues of \(y_j\). The term \emph{tribe} will refer to either such group when the observation being used is specified. A protected test includes a symmetric collar of \(m=\lfloor w/5\rfloor\) neighboring owner widths and periods. Every internal shifted tile query uses its own requisite work history in that collar.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Organization induction]\label{prop:organization}
For every \(j\ge1\), every translated rank-\(j\) grid and every data history with \(j\)-sparse deviations from the prescribed rule in a protected test, assume the lower layers are ordinary and unhalted. Use the true read-only operands. At every protected query with the requisite work and collar:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item\label{org:classification} Each tile is either nominally live or proper dead. Nominally live tiles of different geographical origins cannot overlap. The middle of three matching nominal positive tiles is proper live.
\item\label{org:successor} A proper-dead or an incoming-clear live input has a proper nominal successor. A proper-live input is served.
\item\label{org:issue} The computing tile has \(x_j'=F_j(y_{-},y,y_{+})\), where \(F_j\) is the prescribed rank-\(j\) local map, and the noncomputing tile has \(x_j'=\Dead\). For either kind of dead issue the actual matching occupancy at the new boundary is confined to the exceptional column and is empty if that column is empty. In early Grow it has no genuine extension apart from a short transient from that column. For a positive issue the three central payloads are exact outside the column, \(C_*\) is occupied off a single interval of width at most \(2q\), and surviving incumbents outside the column have a true positive decision. One common interval of width at most \(2q\) contains both the support and tested-payload exceptions.
\item\label{org:advance} In a stencil of nine proper incoming-clear inputs, the central output equals \(F_j\) evaluated on the served triple, including a requested birth.
\end{enumerate}
At rank one the child transition on an unflagged update is the actual native rule. The cut assertion \ref{org:issue} also holds when \(F_j\) is identically \(\Dead\) because of its true halted literal. In a wholly \((j-1)\)-sparse test the rank-\(j\) exceptional column is empty.
\end{proposition}

For the proof, restrict the flag set to the buffered domain and extend it by zero. By the count in the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:transition}, every query of the one-rank argument at a tile, including lower protected calls, service lookahead and shifted-grid rounding, lies within \(611\) owner units of the tile, hence inside the tile's \(m\)-collar enlarged by one unit. That region has extent \(2m+3\le w\) owner units in both axes, so the translate \(Q\) of \(wV^j\) with the same lower corner contains it. Remove the single translate \(J\) of \(3wV^{j-1}\) that Definition~\ref{def:sparse} permits in \(Q\). If the footprint of a lower call meets \(J\), its tile and next row lie within \(m+1\) child units of a point of \(J\) in space, and between \(m+1\) units before and \(m+2\) units after such a point in time, up to an error below \(2(m+1)/P\). The child-grid cells meeting this enlargement of \(J\) form one common column and time interval \(K\times S\), with spans at most \(3w+2m+4\) and \(3w+2m+5\), both less than \(d\). The common span \(3w+2m+4+2\cdot608<d\) of Appendix~\ref{sec:constants} allows padding by \(m+2+608\) child units on every side; it contains this \(K\times S\), and its term \(2\cdot608\) is fixed slack. Lower calls whose footprints miss \(J\) use the lower-rank induction with the actual restricted history. If the restricted deviations in \(Q\) are already \((j-1)\)-sparse, take \(J=K=S=\varnothing\); this is the convention for an invocation that needs no deletion. No raw substitutions of smaller rank are thereby removed, and all actual deviations, including those in \(J\), stay in the history. At the base one uses ordinary native transitions off flagged sites.

The lemmas below establish the implication from rank \(j-1\) to rank \(j\). The regularity used within a structural step is always that of the lower rank. First start ordinary work from an arbitrary input and obtain a common full seed. The two-flank clearance takes a specified old interval as hypothesis, independently of any new issued cut. Establish weak descriptions at clean centers and at general centers, then the strong issue from preceding weak descriptions. Next classify the issued faces and prove genuine source attachment, density, backward inheritance and overlap exclusion. The end repair applied to a proper current input gives service without requiring a positive successor. For a proper-dead input with a requested birth, advance gives a computing target. The strong issue gives the prescribed live result, its incoming clearance follows by the backward inheritance implication, and the positive-successor part of end repair establishes properness. The clearance and startup parts of advance do not use that properness. For a halted literal stop at its strong cut and do not induct through an ordinary layer above it. The additional two-period conclusion will be used for the initialized descent.

A component refers to one exact extended source during Grow and to one ordinary home after normalization. The words \emph{retired strobe} refer to a sampling date after a complete provisional retirement, positioned relative to the scheduled true instruction as specified in each lemma. Ordinary Heal means a fresh complete cycle of provisional attempts and removals, with the in-home restriction. In the terminal withdrawal calculations \(r\) denotes \(r_{\mathrm{last}}\) from Section~\ref{sec:clock}. Write \(C=[12A,P-12A)\) for the ordinary core before the \(4q\) reserve at the issuing cut. The local bounds below may use additional quantities defined in the individual calculations. At searching ranks \(F_j\) is owner-specific through the true external words; no step compares maps of different owners. The served-ancestry consequence of Proposition~\ref{prop:organization}\ref{org:issue} is that every protected live output off the exceptional column (at rank zero: on an unflagged update) has an actual served incumbent or donor within radius one at the preceding row, with the same parent clock and address residues; the native transition and Lemma~\ref{lem:transition} give this consequence at each step.
