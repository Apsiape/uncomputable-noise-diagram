\subsection{Autonomous estimation of the window}\label{sec:receiver}

The windows are \(I_n=(2^{-A_n},2^{1-A_n})\), where \(A_n=8^{n+n_0}\) and the fixed integer \(n_0\) will be chosen last. Set \(p=\varepsilon/2\). In one logical lane the raw probes are previous-row values of physical bits with deterministic proposal zero. They are independent Bernoulli \(p\) in all updated rows. The data and helper roles in a given lane use disjoint offsets, as specified in Section~\ref{sec:embedding}.

The receiver uses 13 private probes for a replicated wall bit, five for a replicated mode bit, and three groups of 59 replicas for wall, mode and work. Propose a wall refresh when the first 12 wall probes are one, adopting the thirteenth bit. Otherwise hold the wall bit. Propose a mode command when the first three mode probes are one, choosing CLEAN if the last two are one and RUN otherwise. Otherwise hold its mode. Independent commands of the two kinds have respective probabilities \(p^5\) and \(p^3(1-p^2)\) at a given date. Decode by plurality with a fixed first-replica tie rule. A decoded register error requires at least 30 flags in its group, with probability at most \(2^{59}(2p)^{30}\). This necessary event refers only to flags and holds whatever the previous contents. Use the proposal of the decoded recurrence for every replica. The raw proposal bit and every replica group occupy private offsets.

Walls are barriers for all work and control storage. A wall's own work state is canonical. Its mode drives the open segment to its right by a speed-one wave. CLEAN blanks every workspace, display, head and launch register reached by the wave. After a continuous CLEAN, a CLEAN-to-RUN command launches one head at the first open site, one step behind the RUN wave. The head moves at most one site in two steps. The mode-edge test uses a one-step delay overwritten from the current mode. When a boundary is absent the same local rules apply, with conflicting head requests blanked and with CLEAN and barriers having priority. RUN while already RUN does not relaunch.

In a clean segment of length \(L\), the head counts its interior sites by shuttling between an advancing marker and a binary counter near the left wall. It accepts \(n\) exactly when
\[
A_n/3\le\log_2 L\le 2A_n.
\]
The bins are disjoint. Integer comparisons suffice, for example \(L^3\ge2^{A_n}\) for the lower bound. Generate candidates until the next lower bound exceeds \(L\). The head writes the result on a boundary track and can copy its binary description to the display. The tape has finitely many tracks, two local end tests, and a default abort on an invalid bin or work overflow. The same head will also compute the stripes in Section~\ref{sec:stripes}. All prepublication algorithms used here cost \(O(L^2\,\mathrm{poly}(\log L))\) rounds, for any reference input. The compact counters and finite template fit in the segment for all sufficiently large valid \(L\). Shuttling a fixed number of times per bit of a counter costs \(O(L\,\mathrm{poly}(\log L))\) per visited site. The hardwired offset \(n_0\) enters only the bin arithmetic. At accepted lengths its contribution to the job-time constant is at most polynomial in \(n_0\), hence at most \(\operatorname{poly}(\log\log L)\); the final increase of \(n_0\), whose windows shrink doubly exponentially, absorbs this factor uniformly and does not create a circular constant choice. The integer comparisons may stop generation as soon as a lower bound is too large for the observed \(L\), so the cost at the accepted indices grows polynomially in \(\log L\) with a fixed template. A fixed allowance covers the launch delay. The estimates below use the room between \(L=O(p^{-3/2})\) and the time budget of order \(p^{-4}\).

The finite-prefix assertion below is used only at observation dates \(t\ge B_0+H+4\), with \(B_0,H\) as in its proof; burn-in or a two-sided stationary helper history supplies those dates.
\begin{lemma}[A correct reader]\label{lem:reader}
For each observation in one lane at a rate in \(I_n\), the receiver has a sufficient correct-job test using innovations in a deterministic rectangle. It passes with probability at least \(1-C\sqrt p\) for all sufficiently small \(p\), uniformly over incoming states. On passing, the observing site lies at distance at least \(\lfloor p^{-1/2}\rfloor\) from the current walls of a cleanly launched segment of length at most \(2\lceil p^{-3/2}\rceil+O(1)\). The correct index and every stipulated publication fit before observation. For private copies of the receiver the tests are independent within one lane. The test for a reader is independent of the reference track values in the sense that the same passing innovations work for every static reference pattern.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use \(W=\lceil p^{-3/2}\rceil\), \(H=\lceil p^{-6}\rceil\), \(B_0=\lceil p^{-14}\rceil\), \(S=\lceil p^{-4}\rceil\) and \(\ell_0'=\lfloor p^{-1/2}\rfloor\). Inspect the site interval of radius \(W+3\) in a prefix of \(B_0\) and suffix of \(H\) rounds, with fixed extra boundary dates for the queried raw row. Require no excessive wall flags in this rectangle and no excessive mode or work flags in the suffix. The probability of a necessary flag event in these sets is at most
\[
O\bigl((W+1)(B_0+H+4)p^{30}\bigr)=O(p^{29/2}).
\]
Work first with the ideal bits determined by commands and subtract this fault bound by a union bound. Every inspected site refreshes its wall within the prefix except with probability \((2W+7)e^{-B_0p^{12}}\). Given refreshes, the last proposals are independent Bernoulli \(p\) bits from the thirteenth probes. The probability of a wall refresh in the suffix is \(O(WHp^{12})=O(p^{9/2})\). The probability of a wall near the root within \(\ell_0'\) or a missing wall within distance \(W\) on either side is \(O(p\ell_0')+2e^{-cpW}\). The resulting length satisfies the prescribed bin inequalities for all \(p\in(2^{-A_n-1},2^{-A_n})\) once \(n_0\) is large. In particular its binary logarithm lies between \(A_n/2-O(1)\) and \(3A_n/2+O(1)\).

The selected left wall depends on wall probes alone. The last CLEAN in the suffix exists and precedes the final \(S\) rounds except with probability \(e^{-cHp^5}+O(Sp^5)\). Conditional on its date, the later rounds have no CLEAN and have independent RUN commands of probability \(p^3(1-p^2)/(1-p^5)\). The first such RUN is more than \(2W+10\) rounds after CLEAN and fewer than \(S/2\) rounds after CLEAN except with probability \(O(Wp^3)+e^{-cSp^3}\). Hence all stale work is blanked, and the uninterrupted run before observation has at least \(S/2\) rounds for the stipulated publication. All inspected work follows the intended computation outside the charged flag event. The sum of the failure probabilities is \(O(\sqrt p)\). The construction selects no wall using work marks, and the sufficient event ignores the values supplied to the head.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Forgetting of full receiver storage]\label{lem:receiver-mixing}
At each sufficiently small fixed \(p>0\), the receiver has exponentially decaying one-slot all-input disagreement under common innovations. This is uniform for every common exogenous reference trajectory. It includes the undecoded physical storage.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Inspect a deterministic interval of radius \(2W+O(1)\) in the lane for the same \(B_0\)-round prefix as in Lemma~\ref{lem:reader}, then eight consecutive \(H\)-round slabs. Allow a fixed number of boundary rounds and discard the first update for commands that query the incoming raw bit. The probability of any required excessive flags is \(O(p^{29/2})\). In ideal bits the prefix produces common wall and mode values for all inputs except with probability \(O(W e^{-cB_0p^{12}})\). For each spatial site the probability of a wall at the prefix end and no refresh in the eight slabs is at least
\[
p\bigl[1-(1-p^{12})^{B_0}\bigr](1-p^{12})^{8H+O(1)}\ge cp.
\]
The events are independent at distinct sites. Thus except with probability \(O(e^{-cpW})\) there is an enduring wall in each of the intervals \([-2W,-W]\) and \([W,2W]\).

A helpful slab has no wall refresh inside the inspected interval, and at every potential left wall there is a CLEAN followed by \(5W+10\) rounds without RUN. The wall failure probability is \(O(p^{9/2})\). Split the slab into order \(p^{-1}\) disjoint subintervals of length of order \(p^{-5}\). In each, the first half has a CLEAN with probability bounded below, and in the following \(5W+10\) rounds RUN arrives with probability \(O(Wp^3)\). These rounds fit in the same subinterval. A union over possible walls gives mode failure \(O(W e^{-c/p})\). The helpfulness tests on the eight slabs use disjoint primitive innovation rows, regardless of which walls endure, so their joint failure probability is \(O(p^{36})\).

In a helpful slab the whole work in every segment between the enduring walls is blanked and becomes common. As long as the two walls persist, even changes of interior boundaries cannot bring in an old discrepancy. A final proposal and common physical replacements give agreement in the raw replica slots as well as in the decoded registers. The argument holds for each common reference stream and uses only noise to certify the conclusion. At a depth \(d_{\mathrm{rec}}=B_0+8H+O(1)\) the all-input nonconstancy coefficient for a fixed slot is \(q_{\mathrm{rec}}=O(p^{29/2})+O(p^{36})\) plus superpolynomially small terms. Taking a finite union covers the finitely many slots queried by a physical symbol.

If a composition of \(k\) such slabs is nonconstant at a site, trace a chain of nonconstant output maps, one per slab. Each map has at most \(2r_0d_{\mathrm{rec}}+1\) possible parent sites for the chain, where \(r_0\) is the fixed physical range. For a fixed chain the failure tests depend on disjoint innovation slabs. The union bound is \(((2r_0d_{\mathrm{rec}}+1)q_{\mathrm{rec}})^k\), which decays exponentially for small \(p\) because \(d_{\mathrm{rec}}=O(p^{-14})\). A finite remainder at the initial end of the time interval does not change the conclusion.
\end{proof}
