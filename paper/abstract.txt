We construct one finite-range cellular automaton on the line with computable disjoint rational windows of uniform replacement noise. Throughout the window indexed by a program, the perturbed automaton is uniformly exponentially ergodic from every initial configuration if the program halts. If it runs forever, a one-site clock observable fails to converge from a specified initial law. The same dichotomy holds at one fixed positive rational noise rate for a computable family of radius-one rules. On the square lattice, the single-rule construction gives one finite-range interaction with local energies zero and one. Its Dobrushin-Lanford-Ruelle specification has a unique Gibbs measure in each halting window of inverse temperature and at least two Gibbs measures in each nonhalting window. The construction uses a hierarchy of fault-tolerant simulations and autonomous spatial signals that encode the noise window.
