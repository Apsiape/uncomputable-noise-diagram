\subsection{Spatial stripes}\label{sec:stripes}

Choose \(D_0>1\) after fixing the data geometry. A radius \(H_n=D_0^{n+2}\) will dominate all prescribed fixed enlargements of the boxes through rank \(n+1\), including the lookup coordinates and collars. At a rate in \(I_n\), \(H_n=p^{-o(1)}\), uniformly with arbitrarily small fixed positive exponent after increasing \(n_0\).

Use an odd number \(m_h\) of independent reference channels \(Y_i\), each with separate probe and replica offsets in the lane. At each site \(Y_i\) refreshes when its first \(b_h\) private probes are one and its next two probes differ. Adopt the first bit of that differing pair, otherwise hold the previous plurality. Use \(2v_h-1\) replicas. An uncommanded decoded change requires at least \(v_h\) flags. True refreshes have rate \(2p^{b_h+1}(1-p)\); their orientation is fair. A refresh produces identical proposals in all copies of the trajectory, and common replacements preserve this equality after refresh. Conditional on refresh in a prescribed interval, the value at the end of the interval is fair: after the first refresh simultaneously complement every fair assigned bit and every subsequent fair replacement bit. At each site and in each channel these operations use private primitive pools.

For reference channel \(i\), a stripe anchor is the right endpoint of the pattern consisting of one followed by \(k_n=\lfloor A_n/8\rfloor\) zeros. At a site \(x\), use the nearest such endpoint \(o_i\) at or to the left of \(x\) within distance \(R_n=2^{\lfloor A_n/4\rfloor}\). Publish zero if there is none. Otherwise publish \(\lfloor(x-o_i)/P^n\rfloor\bmod2\). The head records the last eligible endpoint during a left-to-right scan and checks its distance before publishing. A head reading a stable pattern uses at most \(R_n+k_n+O(1)\) consecutive input sites for a given publication. It computes \(n,k_n,R_n,P^n\) using \(O(\log L)\)-bit counters for an accepted segment of length \(L\). Keeping the last endpoint, detecting runs and writing the outputs adds \(O(L^2\,\mathrm{poly}(\log L))\) rounds for any fixed number of channels. As in Lemma~\ref{lem:reader}, insufficient space causes an abort. On the sufficient event the wall is farther from the observation site than \(R_n+k_n+2\), since \(R_n+k_n+2<\lfloor p^{-1/2}\rfloor\) for large \(n_0\), so the output depends only on the intended reference window. Use an odd number \(s_h\) of private receivers, all reading the same \(Y_i\), and export the coordinatewise majority of their decoded displayed bits (with a fixed default for malformed storage).

\begin{lemma}[Locally static stripes]\label{lem:stripes}
Prescribe \(M_s>0\) first, choose \(b_h\) for the short event, and only then prescribe \(M_l>0\). With these choices there are short and long bad events at every center. For suitable choices of the constants in the order described below, their probabilities are at most \(p^{M_s}\) and \(p^{M_l}\), and their innovation supports lie within radii \(p^{-16}\) and \(p^{-(b_h+5)}\), respectively. Outside both events, throughout the radius-\(H_n\) box each exported channel is static in time and a strict majority of the channels have values \(\lfloor(x-o_i)/P^n\rfloor\bmod2\), with possibly different phases \(o_i\). The conclusion holds for all incoming helper states before the innovation prefix. At each sufficiently small fixed \(p\), under common innovations the probability that the value of a physical helper slot \(s\) steps after an arbitrary start is not the same for all incoming helper states is at most \(C(p)e^{-c(p)s}\). The complete autonomous helper process has a two-sided stationary realization determined by its past innovations.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
At every read in the box use the sufficient reader tests of Lemma~\ref{lem:reader}. They are independent across private copies at that read and work for every static reference pattern. The probability that the majority test fails somewhere is bounded by
\[
O\bigl(H_n^2 C_{s_h}(C\sqrt p)^{(s_h+1)/2}\bigr).
\]
Also charge any reference refresh or excessive replica flag event during the suffix in which the completed jobs can scan the inputs. It suffices to use a space range of width \(O(W+H_n+R_n+k_n)\) and time length \(O(p^{-6}+H_n)\), including fixed row lags. The cost is
\[
O_{m_h}\bigl(p^{-8}(p^{b_h+1}+C_{v_h}p^{v_h})\bigr).
\]
Thus, off this short event, all jobs producing a read in the box use the same time-independent reference values. The prefix for the jobs has depth \(O(p^{-14})\), so the short event has the claimed support. First prescribe \(M_s\). Choose \(b_h>20\), \(v_h\), and \(s_h\) large enough that \(M_s\) is strictly below the exponents in the displayed bounds, allowing slack for \(H_n^2\). Fixed multiplicative factors are absorbed by the final choice of \(n_0\).

For the long event, at the start of the common suffix inspect a snapshot of the necessary references in the spatial neighborhood of radius \(R_n+k_n+O(H_n)\). Require each such register to have refreshed in the preceding \(\lceil p^{-(b_h+3)}\rceil\) steps. The union bound for missing any refresh is superpolynomially small. Conditional on the refresh condition, the snapshot is a product of fair bits, independent across channels and sites, by the private-pool construction and the complement argument. All snapshot values are determined by the finite prefix. For one product-fair row, an anchor ends at a prescribed site with probability \(2^{-k_n-1}\). Require an endpoint in a strip of length \(R_n/3\) just to the left of the whole spatial span of the box, and require no endpoint inside the span. The nearest endpoint to the left of the span then serves all sites. A union bound for endpoints inside and disjoint tests in the strip give a failure bound
\[
O(H_n2^{-k_n})+\exp\bigl(-cR_n2^{-k_n}/(k_n+1)\bigr)=O(p^{1/10}).
\]
The nearest-endpoint conditions apply independently to the \(m_h\) snapshot rows. The probability that a strict majority of rows fail, or that a strict majority of stripe channels is absent, is bounded (for odd \(m_h\)) by \(C_{m_h}(C p^{1/10})^{(m_h+1)/2}\), plus the missing-refresh bound. After fixing \(b_h\), prescribe \(M_l\) and choose \(m_h\) large enough. The dotwise majority over good readers sees the correct locally static function in every channel, including the channels with no single good anchor across the span. This gives the asserted simultaneous statement. The complete long test fits within the claimed support radius.

At fixed \(p\), in a cone of an output site and through dates at least \(t/2\) after initialization, the chance that any needed reference register still has input dependence is at most a polynomial in \(t\) times \(e^{-c_0(p)t/2}\), with the refresh rate \(c_0(p)=2p^{b_h+1}(1-p)\), by the refresh tail and a union over the finite cone. Given agreement of these reads, Lemma~\ref{lem:receiver-mixing} applies to the common, possibly changing, reference values throughout that part of the cone. The sufficient forgetting tests are uniform over those values, so the combined bound uses a union, with no conditioning on the reference history. Absorbing the polynomial into a smaller positive exponential rate and putting \(t=s\), the value of every physical helper slot \(s\) steps after the start is the same for all incoming helper states except with probability at most \(C(p)e^{-c(p)s}\), for suitable \(C(p),c(p)>0\). This exponential bound is summable. On a two-sided iid innovation field, fix a site and date and run the auxiliary process from increasingly early dates. Borel--Cantelli implies eventual agreement for all starts at that site and date. Intersect over the countable site--time lattice. The limits satisfy the update, commute with time shifts of the innovations and depend only on the past. In particular their time-zero row is independent of fresh future innovations.
\end{proof}
