\section{The dynamical alternatives}\label{sec:dynamics}
The initialized nested descent adapts G\'acs \cite[Section~11, Lemmas~11.1--11.2]{gacs86}; the halting cap and noise-selected external operands are used as specified here.

\subsection{Local comparison for the external operands}\label{sec:environment}

\begin{lemma}[Locking the read-only parameter]\label{lem:locking}
At a rate in \(I_n\), a protected comparison contained in a good stripe box uses SEARCH through rank \(n\) and locks at rank \(b=n+1\) on the true initial tape \(U(n)\). For each eligible protected test at these ranks, the conclusion of the organization induction holds for its actual data, with deviations covered by \(E\) from Section~\ref{sec:joint}. Above rank \(b\) the read-only transition is the uniform locked transition. If \(U(n)\) halts, some first finite rank has a transition identically \(\Dead\). If it never halts, all ranks are ordinary.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In a frozen reference box the distinguished vector of a rank-\(j\) tile with left coordinate \(x\) is the base vector at
\[
x+u(1+P+\cdots+P^{j-1}),
\]
zero extended. The formula at rank zero is the original vector at \(x\). At the next promotion, the three samples have coordinates obtained from the three child positions \(u,u+1,u+2\); after the common lower-rank displacement they are spaced by \(P^j\). When \(j<n\), their total span \(2P^j\) is less than \(P^n\). There is at most one flip in each good channel. At \(j=n\), both adjacent samples differ in every good channel, with no restriction on its phase. Thus the strict-majority test first succeeds at this promotion, with counter \(n\), giving the true lock at \(b=n+1\). Minority channels can be arbitrary spatial functions. The reads on the two protected transactions are the actual loads on their continuously served corridors, so the same argument applies to the read-only operands of a represented forged instruction. This use of the lower claims is part of the rank induction: the actual loads consume established lower observations.

To justify the comparison without global phases, take a fixed eligible protected invocation of rank \(j\le b\), including its calls, and choose an anchor at its tile corner. If the anchor were bad, its paint would contain a full \(V^j\) inside the tested rectangle. By Definition~\ref{def:sparse} and the calculation following it, this is incompatible even with \(j\)-sparsity. Choose the fixed \(D_0\) so that \(D_0^{n+2}\) dominates the largest required width or height of every clipped domain and all its sum-of-offset lookups through rank \(b\). This is possible since each such bound is a fixed multiple of \(\max(P,T)^{n+1}\). Extend the majority stripes by their phases and the static minority functions by arbitrary values outside the good box, all constant in time. On the entire clipped domain keep the original physical data history. Every local proposal, every required load and every \(x,y\) observation then has exactly the external values of this comparison; lower service lookahead stays inside its tile. Both the invocation and its lower calls after one excision in the sparsity argument use the same frozen extension. Only the errors already flagged in \(E\) need be charged. For ranks above \(b\), apply the comparison locally wherever a lower invocation through \(b\) is needed. The true tape is the same at all such locks, and the external word at higher ranks is zero. Thus no common phase across separated boxes is needed. The last claims follow from the exact true step and halted guard of Section~\ref{sec:parameter}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An empty cut after halting}\label{sec:cap}
In both lemmas of this subsection, K denotes the first halted rank; here it does not denote the exceptional column of Section~\ref{sec:organization}. All layers strictly below K are ordinary and unhalted; no ordinary induction is continued above it. A protected rank-\(K\) work rectangle is a rank-\(K\) tile with its protected test (Definition~\ref{def:proper}), and its buffered test is that protected test, to which the deviations are restricted as in Section~\ref{sec:organization}.

\begin{lemma}[All-start cut at a halted rank]\label{lem:cap-cut}
Let \(K\ge1\) be the first halted rank. In every protected rank-\(K\) work rectangle whose buffered test is \((K-1)\)-sparse, the output cut has no served live child of the tested clock and address group. This holds for arbitrary initial physical rows when the work and its preceding history lie after initialization.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use the strong issue of Lemma~\ref{lem:ballot} with an empty rank-\(K\) exception. The lower calls still see their actual lower-rank defects. If the tile computes, both unaffected repetitions freshly initialize the evaluator and use the genuine halted literal at the covered loads (Lemma~\ref{lem:locking} for the single rule). The evaluator returns \(\Dead\) on every parsed triple, including the case of a forged nonhalted work tape and all malformed inputs. The dead half of the issuing cut confines matching survivors to the exceptional column, which is empty. If the tile fails to compute, the noncomputing clause gives the same empty cut. These calculations assume no form of the starting row. They apply separately on each translated grid.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Ascent from a live physical site]\label{lem:ascent}
At fixed \(K\ge1\) there are centered tests of fixed multiples of \(V^{j+1}\), \(0\le j<K\), with multipliers independent of \(j\) and \(K\), and a finite common burn-in such that a live physical site after burn-in has at least one failed \(j\)-sparsity test centered there. This holds for all physical starts and the tested field of deviations.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Assume all tests pass. Begin at rank zero and whenever there is a served live site at rank \(j\), trace it to a parent computation. Its clock and residue determine a parent grid. Let \(c\) be the latest cut date of that grid not after the site's date \(t\), and put \(a=c-T\) in child time units. For \(j=0\) each update in the trace is unflagged by the rank-zero test and uses the native rule. For \(j>0\), at each protected trace query a served live output equals its live nominal output. Proposition~\ref{prop:organization}\ref{org:issue} thus gives a computing owner and the native transition on the actual served triple, as in Lemma~\ref{lem:transition}. The live result has a served incumbent or donor at distance at most one on the preceding row. Its clock and address residues preserve the parent phase. Trace \(t-a<2T\) such steps. The ancestor at date \(a\) is a matching child at a parent cut, at spatial displacement less than \(2T\) in child units. At that cut addresses have normalized and the canonical ballot did not make any births.

Use the immediately preceding parent work on the unique spatial home of this child. A \(\Dead\) issue would leave that child absent by the empty-exception strong cut. At rank \(K\) this contradicts Lemma~\ref{lem:cap-cut}. At a smaller rank the issue equals the native map on three actual served inputs. That map on three empty inputs gives empty. Choose a served live operand and continue the ascent from its site, rather than from the issue itself.

The displacement from one served site at rank \(j\) to the next ascent input is less than \(3T\,T^j\) in time and less than \((2T+2P)P^j\) in space. The former pays for the two periods of ancestry and one of work; the latter pays for ancestry and the offsets to the home and its neighboring input. Since \(P,T\ge2\), geometric summation over the ranks \(0,\ldots,j\) bounds the total displacement from the physical site to the rank-\((j+1)\) ascent input by \(6T^{j+1}\) backward in time and by \((4T/P+4)P^{j+1}\) in either spatial direction. Use these bounds as centered half-extents before padding. They bound the intermediate trace positions and the preceding parent work as well: the bound for each step already includes two periods of ancestry, one period of parent work and the horizontal home and operand offsets. Add \((m+2)P^{j+1}+4P^j\) on each spatial side and \((m+2)T^{j+1}+4T^j\) on each temporal side. The padding includes one parent tile extent and its \(m\)-collar enlarged by one parent unit, with the four child rounding units. It therefore contains every protected rank-\(j\) trace query and every selected rank-\((j+1)\) work test by the localization bound in Section~\ref{sec:organization}. The rank-\(j\) sparsity of the centered rectangle passes to each of these restricted tests. In particular the rank-\((j+1)\) cut has an empty exception. The rectangle contains the original site and date, and its multipliers relative to \(V^{j+1}\) depend only on \(P,T,m\). Observations are within the service lookahead established in Section~\ref{sec:organization}. Choose the burn-in greater than the past extent of these finitely many tests, including their required helper innovation prefixes. The observations can include later updates from the actual history. This proves the claim from every start.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exponential forgetting in a halting window}\label{sec:halting}

Couple any two starts using common whole-symbol innovations. At rank-zero halting the data proposal is always \(\Dead\), hence it couples in one tick. Otherwise let \(K\ge1\) be the first halted rank (Lemma~\ref{lem:locking} for the single rule; the hardwired literal for the family) and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:ascent} to both trajectories starting after the common burn-in. For an output site at time \(t\) consider each necessary helper read \((x,s)\) in its finite physical light cone at dates \(s\ge t/2\). The individual disagreement bound has exponent at least \(ct/2\), by Lemma~\ref{lem:stripes}. A union bound over the polynomially many sites, times and slots in this part of the cone gives a bound of the form \(\mathrm{poly}(t)e^{-ct/2}\). In the family no helpers are needed.

On agreement of these helper reads, any remaining differing data site has a preceding differing data input, since a common replacement erases the discrepancy and a common deterministic input yields the same proposal. Such a chain stays in one logical lane. At every disputed vertex some data value is live, as \(\Dead\) is unique. Lemma~\ref{lem:ascent} places every vertex beyond burn-in into the same noise-measurable set of failed tests, independently of which trajectory supplies the live site. A chain lasting to the middle has temporal extent at least \(t/2-O(1)\). Lemma~\ref{lem:joint} bounds its probability by \(C'e^{-c'(t/2-O(1))}\), which is at most \(C''e^{-c't/2}\); the parent steps are nearest or diagonal neighbors in the lane. The helper coupling and the cluster bound use no independence between the two failure events. The fixed finite number of physical slots is covered by a union bound.

Absorb burn-in and polynomial factors into the constants. The resulting one-site total disagreement is at most \(Ce^{-ct}\) for every pair of physical starts. A union bound gives \(C|F|e^{-ct}\) for finite \(F\). Compare to a start sampled from a stationary law, whose existence follows as in Section~\ref{sec:model}. This proves \eqref{eq:uee}, including the full physical symbol.

\subsection{The initialized hierarchy}\label{sec:initialized}

\begin{lemma}[An initialized negative-time comparison]\label{lem:past}
For a nonhalting input there is an initial physical law with a compatible fully occupied aligned comparison before time zero. On its homogeneous ranks, all ranks for the family and ranks \(j\ge b\) for the single rule, the rank-\(j\) site \(i\) at each rank-\(j\) date \(s\le0\) is live with clock \(s\bmod T\) and address \(i\bmod P\). In the fixed-rate family this comparison has no negative-time faults, and the initial law is the point mass at its time-zero row. For the single rule its deviations on negative times lie in the paint, and its time-zero helper row comes from the stationary autonomous realization. For the single rule the comparison is set in every residue lane of Section~\ref{sec:embedding}, in that lane's logical coordinates and with the same aligned skeleton. In both cases every physical data site has age \(0\) at date \(0\), the time-zero data row is deterministic, and positive-time updates are those of the PCA with fresh innovations.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
First construct a clean all-positive history on the site-homogeneous ordinary ranks, all ranks for the family and ranks \(j\ge b\) for the single rule. At a finite top rank \(h\), take the fixed live padding letter of the packet convention of Section~\ref{sec:packets}. At rank-\(h\) date \(-1\), that is at physical time \(-T^h\), put the aligned live row in which site \(i\) has ordinary address \(i\bmod P\), age \(T-1\), that letter as retained letter, active bit one, three copies of that letter in the output banks and default values in the other fields. Its ballot gives an aligned live reset row at rank-\(h\) date \(0\). Apply the native initialization tick to that row to obtain the aligned live row at date \(1\). Use the rows at dates \(-1,0,1\) as the top data. Encode each upper row and unfold each work period between successive upper rows. Lemma~\ref{lem:clean} gives the encoded successor at the next boundary and full occupancy at every intervening date. In each unfolding \(E_j\) gives age zero and ordinary address \(a\) at block position \(a\), and the clock advances by one per tick within a period. At a fixed homogeneous rank \(j<h\) the resulting rows cover physical dates from \(-T^h\) to \(T^h\) on its rank-\(j\) grid. Diagonal compactness over the finite state spaces at individual sites and dates gives a compatible two-sided history on the homogeneous ranks, with clock \(s\bmod T\) and address \(i\bmod P\) at rank-\(j\) site \(i\) and date \(s\). At time zero each fixed spatial coordinate eventually has upper coordinate \(0\) or \(-1\) after iterating \(i\mapsto\lfloor i/P\rfloor\), where the next digit is an endpoint. The complete encoded endpoint is independent of the particular live state. Hence the time-zero boundary is determined at each coordinate by finitely many encoding substitutions. In the family the initial law is the point mass at this time-zero row, followed by the PCA with fresh innovations.

For the single rule continue below rank \(b\) on negative dates. Impose at every aligned boundary the complete \(E_j\) row of the already prescribed upper row. Between boundaries run each ordinary work with its true searching parameter and the environment word from the distinguished representative coordinate and date in the autonomous helper history. Use the stipulated full positive following boundary even where this replaces the last ballot. Before that ballot the ordinary evolution of the full row stays fully occupied. When the parent box is good, its representative operands and the actual addressed loads agree with one frozen environment. The clean identity then implies that any mismatch at the lower forced boundary points to a mismatch between the stipulated upper step and its own true update. Writing \(D_j\) for the sites of deviation of the stipulated rank-\(j\) history from its read-only rule, this gives the recursion: every point of \(D_j\) whose parent is good has a parent discrepancy in \(D_{j+1}\). At the locked rank \(b\) of Lemma~\ref{lem:locking} we have \(D_b=\varnothing\). Induction backward through these at most \(b\) promotions shows that each physical mismatch must have a bad parent anchor in this finite ancestry. Its space and time displacement lie in the fixed-multiple \(H_n\) paint by the sum of the bounded lookup displacements through \(b\). At time zero the data row is the aligned skeleton just obtained, with every scratch field default. Its endpoints at successive levels make it independent of the helper phases. Use the time-zero auxiliary row of Lemma~\ref{lem:stripes}; future innovations are independent of it. Carry out this construction in every residue lane of Section~\ref{sec:embedding}, in that lane's logical coordinates and with the same aligned skeleton. The time-zero helper row serves every lane at once, and each lane's negative-time comparison is a measurable function of the one helper history. The time-zero data rows of all lanes together form one deterministic physical data row with age zero at every site. Thus the negative-time comparison and the actual future join at an allowed initial law.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The nonconvergent clock observable}\label{sec:witness}

\begin{lemma}[Nested tests and the clock witness]\label{lem:clock-witness}
On a nonhalting input, for every physical site \(x\) and every integer \(t\ge0\), the initialized law of Lemma~\ref{lem:past} gives probability greater than \(3/4\) to the event that the data field at \(x\) is live with age \(t\bmod T\) at date \(t\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix \(x\) and \(t\). Construct deterministic nested tests from the observation upward in the initialized aligned grid of the lane of \(x\); in the family this is the aligned grid of the line. For the time span \([a,b')\) of a smaller rectangle of length less than \(T^j/2\), choose \(h=\lfloor a/T^j-1/2\rfloor\). Then the span is contained in \([(h+1/2)T^j,(h+2)T^j)\). For spatial span \([c,e)\) of length less than \(P^j\), choose \(k=\lfloor c/P^j\rfloor\). It fits in the adjacent two rank-\(j\) homes \(k,k+1\). Put \(M_0=\lfloor2w/5\rfloor\). In rank-\(j\) owner units take the new test with spatial span \([k-M_0,k+2+M_0)\) and temporal span \([h-M_0,h+2+M_0)\), enlarged by four child units at each end: \(4P^{j-1}\) rank-zero sites of the lane at each spatial end and \(4T^{j-1}\) ticks at each temporal end. This includes the six inputs around the central pair, the preceding work, the two forward periods and the collar of Proposition~\ref{prop:organization}. In particular it contains the region \(W_0\) of Lemma~\ref{lem:two-period} for the central pair \(k,k+1\) and the work interval in period \(h\), which lies in \([k-m-3,k+m+5)\times[h-m-2,h+m+1)\) in owner units, because \(M_0\ge2m\ge m+3\). Its width and height are less than \(wP^j,wT^j\), also less than \(P^{j+1},T^{j+1}/2\), respectively. Thus each new test admits the next choice of covering homes.

The fixed clock satisfies \(T>8M_0+40\), which is equivalent to \((2M_0+10)T^{r-1}<T^r/4\) at every rank \(r\ge1\). Choose a sufficiently large finite top rank \(r\ge1\), above the lock when applicable, with \(4(t+2)<T^r\). The preceding smaller test contains the observation and has height less than \(T^r/4\), so it lies in \((-T^r/4,T^r/2)\) in time; for a one-site starting observation the left endpoint is \(t\). In both cases the left endpoint lies in \([-T^r/2,T^r/2)\), so the floor prescription gives \(h=-1\); use the same spatial covering rule. All six top inputs at \(-T^r\), even with lookahead at most \(T^r-1\), are read on strictly negative dates. At each aligned child work boundary the comparison row is the exact encoding of the corresponding upper row, and throughout each intervening work interval its child rows have full occupancy and the scheduled residues. Apply Definition~\ref{def:observations} inductively from rank zero within the negative-time observation cones. The three central packet copies at the bottom of each tile decode the comparison symbol, and its service sample has all \(P\) matching children by the lower-rank assertion. Thus the six actual top inputs are the prescribed live comparison symbols in one tribe. At rank \(r\) the rule on their triples is the homogeneous comparison rule, so its two prescribed results are the live comparison symbols at rank-\(r\) date zero, with the next clock and address residues.

Require \((j-1)\)-sparsity of the rank-\(j\) test at all these finitely many ranks. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:two-period} to the top test. At a rank \(j>1\) the conclusion supplies the six served live inputs of the next test down and the served live successors of its two central owners, with the stipulated residues. These sites and dates lie in the covered pair of homes and time interval by the nesting construction. Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:organization}\ref{org:issue} at rank \(j-1\) to those two owners. Their protected tests lie in the rank-\((j-1)\) test since \(M_0\ge m+1\). Each served live successor equals its live nominal value, so each owner computes, and that nominal issue is the prescribed transition on its actual served triple. This supplies the live-output premise for the next application of Lemma~\ref{lem:two-period}. At rank \(1\) the conclusion gives the root physical site's live value with the initialized age directly. The tests depend only on the coordinates of the observation in its lane's aligned grid, and the descent applies to any homes of that grid. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:joint} to these deterministic tests. For the fixed-rate family use the raw flags, which dominate all deviations of the comparison. For the single rule require sparsity of the set \(E\) of Section~\ref{sec:joint}. It carries paint at every date and dominates the deviations of the joined history, including those of the negative-time comparison of Lemma~\ref{lem:past}. At each eligible protected invocation through rank \(b=n+1\) in the descent and its lower calls, apply Lemma~\ref{lem:locking} with that invocation's protected test and its sparsity hypothesis. Its anchor is good and its addressed loads agree with the local frozen comparison. Above \(b\) the locked transition is uniform, and any needed comparisons through \(b\) are made locally at their own protected invocations. In both constructions Lemma~\ref{lem:joint} bounds the sum of the failure probabilities of the prescribed tests by a number strictly less than \(1/4\), uniformly in their translations. For the single rule this estimate applies in each residue lane by translation invariance of the physical rule and the uniform bounds for the helper events. This proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

For the clopen event that the origin is live of age zero, the probability is greater than \(3/4\) at dates congruent to zero modulo \(T\) and less than \(1/4\) at dates congruent to one. Hence this initial law gives failure of weak convergence. Write \(\nu_t\) for the row law at date \(t\). For \(i\in\{0,\ldots,T-1\}\), take the phase means \(N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\nu_{i+nT}\). Along one common subsequence let their weak limits be \(\lambda_i\). The Feller property and telescoping of the phase means give \(\lambda_iP_\varepsilon^T=\lambda_i\) and \(\lambda_iP_\varepsilon=\lambda_{(i+1)\bmod T}\). The clopen event separates \(\lambda_0\) and \(\lambda_1\). Their full equal phase average is stationary for \(P_\varepsilon\). This leaves open multiplicity of stationary row laws for \(P_\varepsilon\).

One rule is used for the entire computable sequence of rational windows. The estimates above hold at every \(\varepsilon\in I_n\), proving Theorem A. For the family the radius-one table is effectively enumerable from the initial tape on \(n\). The geometry and both raw-flag thresholds in Lemma~\ref{lem:joint} have no word-width dependence. Fix one rational \(\varepsilon_*>0\) below those thresholds, for instance a sufficiently small inverse power of two. The halted rank enters the convergence constants only, and the nonhalting witness uses the same threshold for every family member. This proves Theorem C.
