\section{The dynamical alternatives}\label{sec:dynamics}
The initialized nested descent adapts G\'acs \cite[Section~11, Lemmas~11.1--11.2]{gacs86}; the halting cap and noise-selected external operands are used as specified here.

\subsection{Local comparison for the external operands}\label{sec:environment}

\begin{lemma}[Locking the read-only parameter]\label{lem:locking}
At a rate in \(I_n\), a protected comparison contained in a good stripe box uses SEARCH through rank \(n\) and locks at rank \(b=n+1\) on the true initial tape \(U(n)\). For each eligible protected test at these ranks, the conclusion of the organization induction holds for its actual data, with deviations covered by \(E\) from Section~\ref{sec:joint}. Above rank \(b\) the read-only transition is the uniform locked transition. If \(U(n)\) halts, some first finite rank has a transition identically \(\Dead\). If it never halts, all ranks are ordinary.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In a frozen reference box the distinguished vector of a rank-\(j\) tile with left coordinate \(x\) is the base vector at
\[
x+u(1+P+\cdots+P^{j-1}),
\]
zero extended. The formula at rank zero is the original vector at \(x\). At the next promotion, the three samples have coordinates obtained from the three child positions \(u,u+1,u+2\); after the common lower-rank displacement they are spaced by \(P^j\). When \(j<n\), their total span \(2P^j\) is less than \(P^n\). There is at most one flip in each good channel. At \(j=n\), both adjacent samples differ in every good channel, with no restriction on its phase. Thus the strict-majority test first succeeds at this promotion, with counter \(n\), giving the true lock at \(b=n+1\). Minority channels can be arbitrary spatial functions. The reads on the two protected transactions are the actual loads on their continuously served corridors, so the same argument applies to the read-only operands of a represented forged instruction. This use of the lower claims is part of the rank induction: the actual loads consume established lower observations.

To justify the comparison without global phases, take a fixed eligible protected invocation of rank \(j\le b\), including its calls, and choose an anchor at its tile corner. If the anchor were bad, its paint would contain a full \(V^j\) inside the tested rectangle. By Definition~\ref{def:sparse} and the calculation following it, this is incompatible even with \(j\)-sparsity. Choose the fixed \(D_0\) so that \(D_0^{n+2}\) dominates the largest required width or height of every clipped domain and all its sum-of-offset lookups through rank \(b\). This is possible since each such bound is a fixed multiple of \(\max(P,T)^{n+1}\). Extend the majority stripes by their phases and the static minority functions by arbitrary values outside the good box, all constant in time. On the entire clipped domain keep the original physical data history. Every local proposal, every required load and every \(x,y\) observation then has exactly the external values of this comparison; lower service lookahead stays inside its tile. Both the invocation and its lower calls after one excision in the sparsity argument use the same frozen extension. Only the errors already flagged in \(E\) need be charged. For ranks above \(b\), apply the comparison locally wherever a lower invocation through \(b\) is needed. The true tape is the same at all such locks, and the external word at higher ranks is zero. Thus no common phase across separated boxes is needed. The last claims follow from the exact true step and halted guard of Section~\ref{sec:parameter}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An empty cut after halting}\label{sec:cap}
In both lemmas of this subsection, K denotes the first halted rank. All layers strictly below K are ordinary and unhalted; no ordinary induction is continued above it.

\begin{lemma}[All-start cut at a halted rank]\label{lem:cap-cut}
Let \(K\ge1\) be the first halted rank. In every protected rank-\(K\) work rectangle whose buffered test is \((K-1)\)-sparse, the output cut has no served live child of the tested clock and address group. This holds for arbitrary initial physical rows when the work and its preceding history lie after initialization.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use the strong issue of Lemma~\ref{lem:ballot} with an empty rank-\(K\) exception. The lower calls still see their actual lower-rank defects. If the tile computes, both unaffected repetitions freshly initialize the evaluator and use the genuine halted literal at the covered loads (Lemma~\ref{lem:locking} for the single rule). The evaluator returns \(\Dead\) on every parsed triple, including the case of a forged nonhalted work tape and all malformed inputs. The dead half of the issuing cut confines matching survivors to the exceptional column, which is empty. If the tile fails to compute, the noncomputing clause gives the same empty cut. These calculations assume no form of the starting row. They apply separately on each translated grid.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Ascent from a live physical site]\label{lem:ascent}
At fixed \(K\ge1\) there are centered tests of fixed multiples of \(V^{j+1}\), \(0\le j<K\), and a finite common burn-in such that a live physical site after burn-in has at least one failed \(j\)-sparsity test centered there. This holds for all physical starts and the tested field of deviations.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Assume all tests pass. Begin at rank zero and whenever there is a served live site at rank \(j\), trace it to a parent computation. Its clock and residue determine a parent grid. Let \(c\) be the latest cut date of that grid not after the site's date \(t\), and put \(a=c-T\) in child time units. A served live output has a nominal live output. Off the exception the output has an actual served incumbent or donor at the preceding date and at spatial distance at most one; its clock and residue preserve the parent phase. A noncomputing owner or a triple of dead inputs cannot issue the live output. These facts follow from the native step at rank zero and from Proposition~\ref{prop:organization} above it, with the conventions of Lemma~\ref{lem:transition}. Trace \(t-a<2T\) such steps. The ancestor at date \(a\) is a matching child at a parent cut, at spatial displacement less than \(2T\) in child units. At that cut addresses have normalized and the canonical ballot did not make any births.

Use the immediately preceding parent work on the unique spatial home of this child. A \(\Dead\) issue would leave that child absent by the empty-exception strong cut. At rank \(K\) this contradicts Lemma~\ref{lem:cap-cut}. At a smaller rank the issue equals the native map on three actual served inputs. That map on three empty inputs gives empty. Choose a served live operand and continue the ascent from its site, rather than from the issue itself.

The displacement from one served site to the next ascent input is less than \(3T\,T^j\) in time and less than \((2T+2P)P^j\) in space. The former pays for the two periods of ancestry and one of work; the latter pays for ancestry and the offsets to the home and its neighboring input. Geometric summation through rank \(j\) bounds the total by \(6T^{j+1}\) and \((4T/P+4)P^{j+1}\) in the two directions. Enlarge the centered tests by the fixed one-rank work and collar multipliers to contain all adaptively selected tests. Observations are within the service lookahead established in Section~\ref{sec:organization}. Choose the burn-in greater than the past extent of these finitely many tests, including their required helper innovation prefixes. The observations can include later updates from the actual history. This proves the claim from every start.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exponential forgetting in a halting window}\label{sec:halting}

Couple any two starts using common whole-symbol innovations. At rank-zero halting the data proposal is always \(\Dead\), hence it couples in one tick. Otherwise let \(K\ge1\) be the first halted rank (Lemma~\ref{lem:locking} for the single rule; the hardwired literal for the family) and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:ascent} to both trajectories starting after the common burn-in. For an output site at time \(t\) consider each necessary helper read \((x,s)\) in its finite physical light cone at dates \(s\ge t/2\). The individual disagreement bound has exponent at least \(ct/2\), by Lemma~\ref{lem:stripes}. A union bound over the polynomially many sites, times and slots in this part of the cone gives a bound of the form \(\mathrm{poly}(t)e^{-ct/2}\). In the family no helpers are needed.

On agreement of these helper reads, any remaining differing data site has a preceding differing data input, since a common replacement erases the discrepancy and a common deterministic input yields the same proposal. Such a chain stays in one logical lane. At every disputed vertex some data value is live, as \(\Dead\) is unique. Lemma~\ref{lem:ascent} places every vertex beyond burn-in into the same noise-measurable set of failed tests, independently of which trajectory supplies the live site. A chain lasting to the middle has temporal extent at least \(t/2-O(1)\). Lemma~\ref{lem:joint} bounds its probability by \(C'e^{-c't}\); the parent steps are nearest or diagonal neighbors in the lane. The helper coupling and the cluster bound use no independence between the two failure events. The fixed finite number of physical slots is covered by a union bound.

Absorb burn-in and polynomial factors into the constants. The resulting one-site total disagreement is at most \(Ce^{-ct}\) for every pair of physical starts. A union bound gives \(C|F|e^{-ct}\) for finite \(F\). Compare to a start sampled from a stationary law, whose existence follows as in Section~\ref{sec:model}. This proves \eqref{eq:uee}, including the full physical symbol.

\subsection{The initialized hierarchy}\label{sec:initialized}

\begin{lemma}[An initialized negative-time comparison]\label{lem:past}
For a nonhalting input there is an initial physical law with a compatible fully occupied aligned comparison before time zero. In the fixed-rate family this comparison has no negative-time faults. For the single rule its deviations on negative times lie in the paint, and its time-zero helper row comes from the stationary autonomous realization. In both cases positive-time updates are those of the PCA with fresh innovations.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
First construct a clean all-positive history on the site-homogeneous ordinary ranks, all ranks for the family and ranks \(j\ge b\) for the single rule. At a finite top rank \(h\), at time \(-T^h\) put the aligned live row with addresses \(i\bmod P\), age \(T-1\), active bit one, three identical live output padding letters and default other fields. Its first step votes live and resets, and its second step holds live during initialization. Descend through all lower homogeneous ranks by encoding and unfolding these work periods. Lemma~\ref{lem:clean} yields the requisite outputs and keeps all prescribed intermediate inputs live. For any fixed finite collection at fixed ranks the discrete state spaces are finite. Diagonal compactness as \(h\) tends to infinity gives a compatible two-sided history on the homogeneous ranks. At time zero each fixed spatial coordinate eventually has upper coordinate \(0\) or \(-1\) after iterating \(i\mapsto\lfloor i/P\rfloor\), where the next digit is an endpoint. The complete encoded endpoint is independent of the particular live state. Hence the time-zero boundary is determined at each coordinate by finitely many encoding substitutions.

For the single rule continue below rank \(b\) on negative dates. Impose at every aligned boundary the complete \(E_j\) row of the already prescribed upper row. Between boundaries run each ordinary work with its true searching parameter and the environment word from the distinguished representative coordinate and date in the autonomous helper history. Use the stipulated full positive following boundary even where this replaces the last ballot. Before that ballot the ordinary evolution of the full row stays fully occupied. When the parent box is good, its representative operands and the actual addressed loads agree with one frozen environment. The clean identity then implies that any mismatch at the lower forced boundary points to a mismatch between the stipulated upper step and its own true update. Writing \(D_j\) for the sites of deviation of the stipulated rank-\(j\) history from its read-only rule, this gives the recursion: every point of \(D_j\) whose parent is good has a parent discrepancy in \(D_{j+1}\). At the locked rank \(b\) of Lemma~\ref{lem:locking} we have \(D_b=\varnothing\). Induction backward through these at most \(b\) promotions shows that each physical mismatch must have a bad parent anchor in this finite ancestry. Its space and time displacement lie in the fixed-multiple \(H_n\) paint by the sum of the bounded lookup displacements through \(b\). At time zero the data row is the aligned skeleton just obtained, with every scratch field default. Its endpoints at successive levels make it independent of the helper phases. Use the time-zero auxiliary row of Lemma~\ref{lem:stripes}; future innovations are independent of it. Thus the negative-time comparison and the actual future join at an allowed initial law.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The nonconvergent clock observable}\label{sec:witness}

\begin{lemma}[Nested tests and the clock witness]\label{lem:clock-witness}
On a nonhalting input, the initialized law of Lemma~\ref{lem:past} has probability greater than \(3/4\) of a live symbol with age \(t\bmod T\) at the origin at every integer date \(t\ge0\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Construct deterministic nested tests from the observation upward in the initialized aligned grid. For the time span \([a,b')\) of a smaller rectangle of length less than \(T^j/2\), choose \(h=\lfloor a/T^j-1/2\rfloor\). Then the span is contained in \([(h+1/2)T^j,(h+2)T^j)\). For spatial span \([c,e)\) of length less than \(P^j\), choose \(k=\lfloor c/P^j\rfloor\). It fits in the adjacent two rank-\(j\) homes \(k,k+1\). Put \(M_0=\lfloor2w/5\rfloor\). In owner units take the new test with spatial span \([k-M_0,k+2+M_0)\) and temporal span \([h-M_0,h+2+M_0)\), allowing four extra fine rounding units at each end. This includes the six inputs around the central pair, the preceding work, the two forward periods and the collar of Proposition~\ref{prop:organization}. Its width and height are less than \(wP^j,wT^j\), also less than \(P^{j+1},T^{j+1}/2\), respectively. Thus each new test admits the next choice of covering homes.

Choose a sufficiently large finite top rank \(r\) above the lock when applicable, so \(4(t+2)<T^r\) and \((2M_0+10)T^{r-1}<T^r/4\). At that rank choose \(h=-1\). The preceding smaller test contains the observation and lies in \([-T^r/2,T^r)\) in time; use the same spatial choice. All six top inputs at \(-T^r\), even with lookahead at most \(T^r-1\), are read on strictly negative dates. They have the stipulated served live values of the comparison, and the two actual prescribed successors are live.

Require \((j-1)\)-sparsity of the rank-\(j\) test at all these finitely many ranks. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:two-period} to the top test. Its served output in the two covering homes and periods includes the six actual served inputs and the two positive successors needed for the next test down. By the strong issue, the latter have the prescribed positive transitions with the needed residues. Continue until the root physical site is served with the initialized age. The tests were chosen using only geometric coordinates. Their total failure probability is less than \(1/4\) by Lemma~\ref{lem:joint}, using raw flags alone in the family. Positive-time flags together with negative-time paint dominate all deviations of the comparison. This proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

For the clopen event that the origin is live of age zero, the probability is greater than \(3/4\) at dates congruent to zero modulo \(T\) and less than \(1/4\) at dates congruent to one. Hence this initial law gives failure of weak convergence. The phase-separated row laws obtained by averaging along one phase are invariant under \(P_\varepsilon^T\). Their equal phase average is stationary for \(P_\varepsilon\). This argument leaves open multiplicity of stationary row laws for the time-one kernel.

One rule is used for the entire computable sequence of rational windows. The estimates above hold at every \(\varepsilon\in I_n\), proving Theorem A. For the family the radius-one table is effectively enumerable from the initial tape on \(n\). The geometry and both raw-flag thresholds in Lemma~\ref{lem:joint} have no word-width dependence. Fix one rational \(\varepsilon_*>0\) below those thresholds, for instance a sufficiently small inverse power of two. The halted rank enters the convergence constants only, and the nonhalting witness uses the same threshold for every family member. This proves Theorem C.
